Is Lotara free?

Lotara is free to download. Lotara Pro unlocks everything, with a free week to try it first.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Lotara works on iPhone on its own. An Apple Watch adds heart rate during workouts, sleep tracking and controls on your wrist.

How accurate is the food scan?

Lotara gives you a careful estimate from your photo, and you can correct any item or amount before you log it. Barcodes and food labels are read exactly.

Where does my data go?

Your health data stays yours. AI features only send what they need, and only after you allow it. Read the privacy policy for the details.
