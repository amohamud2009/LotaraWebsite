What can I use Lotara for?+

Lotara supports people working on habits such as scrolling, vaping, or drinking, alongside mood check-ins, journaling, meditation, and sleep. It’s a wellness companion, not medical care.

What happens if I have a difficult day?+

You can log what happened and keep going. Lotara’s approach makes room for missed days and looks at your patterns over time.

Can I start for free?+

Yes. Core tools are free. Premium adds Lotara Intelligence, advanced analytics, and reports. See the App Store for current subscription pricing and trial terms.

Do I need an Apple Watch?+

You can use Lotara’s core tools on your iPhone. Apple Health sync is optional and adds health context when data is available.
