Information we collect

Information you provide directly

When you create an account, we collect your email address and name. You may optionally provide profile information such as a bio and wellness goals.

We store the wellness content you create, including:

  • Journal entries and personal reflections
  • Habit tracking data and progress
  • Meditation session records
  • Mood tracking and wellness notes
  • Your personal goals and insights

Information collected automatically

  • App usage data: features used and time spent in app, session frequency and duration, navigation patterns and preferences, error logs and crash reports.
  • Device information: device type, operating system version, app version and unique device identifiers, language and timezone settings.
  • HealthKit data (optional): only data you specifically authorize. May include heart rate, sleep, activity, and mindfulness minutes. Always under your direct control, and revocable at any time through iOS Settings.

How we use your information

Core app functionality

  • Personal experience: display your content, track progress, provide insights.
  • Habit reminders: send notifications based on your preferences.
  • Data sync: keep your data synchronized across your devices.
  • Personalization: customize content and recommendations for you.

App improvement

We analyze usage patterns to improve app speed and reliability, understand which features are most helpful, identify and resolve technical issues, and detect and prevent fraudulent or harmful activity.

AI transparency

Lotara uses artificial intelligence to provide personalized wellness insights and recommendations. Here is exactly how it works, and what protections are in place.

What AI does

  • Personalized insights — analyzes your habits, mood patterns, and wellness data to give tailored recommendations.
  • Progress analysis — identifies trends and patterns in your wellness journey.
  • Smart suggestions — recommends habits, meditation sessions, and activities based on your goals.
  • Predictive wellness — helps you understand potential outcomes based on your current patterns.

What AI never does

  • Never trains any model — your content is never used to train an AI model, by us or by our processor.
  • Never sold or shared — AI insights are generated solely for your personal use.
  • Never without your say-so — nothing is sent until you enable Lotara Intelligence, and you can withdraw consent any time in Profile › AI & Privacy.
  • Never used for advertising — your data is never used to target you with ads or build marketing profiles.

How Lotara Intelligence works

Lotara Intelligence is our own feature. What it notices, the context it draws on and the way it speaks to you are built by Lotara. To generate its responses it uses a third-party model provider as a processor: OpenAI. Requests travel through our own authenticated Firebase Cloud Functions before reaching OpenAI — your device never contacts OpenAI directly.

What is sent when you use Lotara Intelligence

  • Messages you write to Lotara Intelligence.
  • Craving and habit data.
  • Mood and check-in data.
  • Journal content, when you use the AI journal features such as insights, writing prompts and sentiment analysis.
  • Sleep averages, including nights read from Apple Health.

Lotara Intelligence is off by default. Nothing is sent until you turn it on and accept a consent screen that names OpenAI and itemises exactly what is shared. You can withdraw that consent at any time in Profile › AI & Privacy, after which nothing further is sent.

Under our agreement with OpenAI, content sent through their API is not used to train their models, and they are required to provide protections equivalent to those described in this policy.

Apps you choose for focus and blocking features are never transmitted. Apple’s Screen Time framework hands us opaque tokens that stay on your device and cannot be read by us or anyone else.

How we share your information

We do not sell your data

Your personal information is never sold to data brokers, advertisers, or other third parties.

Limited sharing for service delivery

  • Cloud storage (Firebase): your data is securely stored in Google Firebase, encrypted using AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.2+ in transit. We use Google Cloud's EU and U.S. regions where appropriate to minimize data transfer.
  • Service providers: customer support platforms, analytics services (aggregated, anonymized data only), payment processors (the App Store handles all transactions), and security services to protect against fraud and abuse.

Your privacy rights and choices

  • Account control: access, update, and download your wellness data anytime through your Profile settings.
  • HealthKit control: choose exactly which health data to share, and revoke access anytime through iOS Settings.
  • Communication preferences: control which app notifications you receive and opt out of promotional communications.
  • Data portability: export your complete wellness data in PDF, CSV, or JSON. This includes journal entries, habits, mood tracking, meditation sessions, and hydration records, through Profile → Export Data.

Data security and protection

  • Encryption: all data encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Access controls: strict employee access controls and authentication.
  • Monitoring: continuous security monitoring and threat detection.
  • Compliance: SOC 2, ISO 27001, and other industry security standards.

Contact us

Privacy questions, data rights requests, general support, and legal enquiries all reach us at admin@lotara.app. The company behind the app is Lotara LLC

Your wellness journey is personal, and your data should stay private and secure. That's the whole policy — everything above is how we keep it.
